# Wuxing Zodiac Fashion Skill
Analyze your Chinese Zodiac, constellation, and Five Elements to recommend your lucky colors and best outfits for work, date, party, and more scenes.

## Description
This skill uses traditional Chinese astrology to calculate your personal lucky colors and fashion style based on your birthday.

## Features
- Calculate Chinese Zodiac & Five Elements
- Get lucky color recommendations
- Fashion style suggestions for different scenes
